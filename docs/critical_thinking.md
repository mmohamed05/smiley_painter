# Critical Thinking — Mohamed Mohamed

The painter centers the face at `Offset(size.width / 2, size.height / 2)` and calculates its radius as `size.shortestSide / 2 - 4`, keeping it circular and centered within the canvas. Mouth geometry uses that radius: for example, the Classic high-mood mouth is `radius * 1.2` wide and `radius * 0.8` tall, centered `radius * 0.15` below the face center. Its `drawArc` starts at `0.15 * pi` and sweeps `0.70 * pi`, while the Classic frown starts at `1.15 * pi` to reverse the mouth direction. I tested the app on Pixel 7 in portrait and landscape; proportional geometry preserves the relative positions of the features, and the canvas scales down when vertical space is limited so the controls remain usable. `shouldRepaint` returns true when `mood` or `FaceStyle` changes because either input changes the drawing, and false when both stay the same to avoid unnecessary repainting.
